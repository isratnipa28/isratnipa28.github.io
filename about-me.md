## **About Me** 

**Who I Am**

I'm Israt Jahan Nipa, a Software Quality Assurance Engineer based in Bangkok, Thailand, originally from Bangladesh. I work at the intersection of software testing and artificial intelligence, with a growing focus on bringing AI/ML thinking into how I approach quality engineering. I'm detail-oriented, analytical, and driven by a self-directed curiosity to keep learning — whether that's a new testing framework or a new machine learning concept.

**Education**

I hold an M.Eng. in Information and Communication Technology from the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand (Aug 2024 – May 2026), supported by the Royal Thai Government's His Majesty the King's Scholarship. My thesis, "Revisiting Spatio-Temporal Complexity in Federated IoT Systems: An Empirical Benchmarking Study," involved building and evaluating a federated learning framework on the Edge-IIoT dataset for resource-constrained IoT security systems.

Before that, I completed a B.Sc. in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at Daffodil International University, Dhaka, Bangladesh (2019–2023), graduating with a 3.98/4.0 GPA on a full merit scholarship. My undergraduate thesis explored hybrid deep learning models for early-stage skin lesion/cancer classification.

**Work Experience**

I began my career as a Software Quality Assurance Engineer at CrackTech Limited (Sep 2023 – Aug 2024), where I reviewed requirements, designed and executed manual, regression, functional, UI/UX, API, and mobile test cases, and used SQL-based validation to investigate and reproduce defects. I collaborated closely with developers, designers, and business stakeholders to support timely, quality releases across multiple parallel projects.

During my graduate studies, I also worked as a Student Assistant in Communications & Digital Outreach at AIT, supporting ICT events and maintaining departmental digital platforms in an international, English-medium environment.

**Where I'm Headed**

My QA background gave me a strong foundation in manual testing, defect analysis, and quality processes, but through my graduate research in federated learning and IoT security, I've developed a deep interest in how AI/ML systems are validated and tested — a natural extension of QA thinking into a newer, more technically complex domain. I'm now expanding into test automation (currently learning Playwright and API automation with Python) while combining that with my ML research background, aiming to grow into roles that bridge quality engineering and AI systems — whether that's ML QA, automation engineering, or quality-focused AI research roles.
