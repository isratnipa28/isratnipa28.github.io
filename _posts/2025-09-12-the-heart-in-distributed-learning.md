When I read the dataset, I did not just see numbers. I saw a network of tiny signals, each one carrying a story about a moment in time.

That is why spatial-temporal graph neural networks feel so alive. They listen to how features move together, how patterns are woven across time and relationship.

In this story, I explore how my PerFed-STGNN design treats each IoT traffic feature like a friend in a conversation — and how the model learns from their whispers and rhythms.

> The most human part of machine learning is not the code. It is learning to listen to the silent structure inside the data.
