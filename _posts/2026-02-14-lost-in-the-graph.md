A graph is not only a mathematical object. It is a map of connections, of trust, of how one feature leans toward another.

In the work on PerFed-STGNN, I felt the model learning the language of relationships: when traffic spikes happen together, when a protocol and a timing pattern belong to the same story.

This post is about being lost in those connections, and finding the quiet beauty of structure in data.

> The graph is where the system learns to care about context.
