# Seven Days at IETF 122 Bangkok: The Most Inspiring Conference I've Ever Attended
## Highlights and key takeaways from attending IETF 122 in Bangkok, connecting with global network engineers.

Attending the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF 122) conference in Bangkok provided a front-row seat to the engineering consensus processes that shape the global internet infrastructure.

## Where Internet Standards Are Born

The IETF is the premier standards organization for the internet, producing RFCs (Request for Comments) that govern protocols like TLS, IPv6, BGP, and DNS.

```
Working Group Proposal -> Internet-Draft -> Peer Review & Debate -> RFC Standard
```

Observing working group sessions highlighted the rigorous debate required to achieve **rough consensus and running code**.

> **Key Takeaway**: Global internet standards rely on open collaboration, empirical protocol testing, and relentless attention to security and interoperability.

## Connecting Standards with AI and Privacy Research

Interacting with network architects reinforced the importance of aligning AI research with established networking standards. As we deploy **Federated Learning** and **Edge AI** across global networks, building protocols that respect existing network topologies and security frameworks is essential.
