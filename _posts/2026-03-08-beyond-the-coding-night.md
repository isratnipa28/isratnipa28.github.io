Some of my deepest insights arrived late, when the lab was quiet and the code was still humming in the background.

This post is not about results. It is about the moments between experiments: the doubts, the tiny victories, and the feeling of building something meaningful.

I wrote it after reading the complete walkthrough and realizing that every line of code carries the patience of an afternoon and the hope of a better tomorrow.

> The best work is the work you can feel, even when the screen is dark.
