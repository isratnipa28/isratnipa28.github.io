There is a promise in personalization: that a shared model can still learn the unique heartbeat of each edge device.

In my work, LoRA adapters became the warm thread connecting global knowledge to local experience. The base model stays calm and general, while a tiny personalized layer learns the taste of a single client's data.

This post is my reflection on the balance between being universal and being locally true.

> A model that respects local nuance is a model that listens without losing its way.
