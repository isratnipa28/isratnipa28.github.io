# What It's Like to Do a Master's on a Royal Thai Scholarship as a Bangladeshi Student
## Reflections on securing the His Majesty the King's Scholarship and moving to Thailand for graduate studies.

Receiving the His Majesty the King's Royal Thai Government Scholarship to pursue a Master's degree at the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT) marked a pivotal milestone in my academic journey.

## Navigating the Graduate Research Landscape

Moving from Bangladesh to Thailand to join an international research community brought both immense opportunity and rigorous academic demands. AIT's diverse student body represents over 40 countries, creating a unique environment for collaborative problem-solving.

> **Key Takeaway**: Graduate research is not merely about taking advanced courses—it is about learning to formulate novel research questions, execute rigorous experiments, and contribute original knowledge to the global scientific community.

## Bridging Software Engineering and AI Research

My transition into Master's research was guided by a clear goal: applying software engineering discipline to emerging challenges in **Federated Learning** and **IoT Security**.

The scholarship provided the platform, mentorship, and compute infrastructure necessary to conduct high-impact research, laying the groundwork for peer-reviewed journal publications and global AI competition entries.
