I have been thinking about what it means for data to choose privacy.

In the Edge-IIoTset project, the numbers are more than examples. They are fragments of hidden lives in smart devices — their rhythms, their noise, their vulnerability.

Federated learning lets the data stay where it belongs. It invites the system to learn around it, not from inside of it.

This post is a quiet reminder: the best AI systems are not only accurate. They are protective.

> When data is safe, the model can be brave.
